Good to know

Richfield leaves and yard waste.

Richfield's Service Department picks up brush and leaves. Here's how it works.

  • Brush goes by quadrant, once a monthHave it at the curb by 7 AM on the Monday of your pickup week. Once they've been by, they won't be back until the next month.
  • Leaves have no set scheduleThe Service Department picks up leaves in the fall as needed.
  • Free wood chips and leaf compostThe village turns the brush into wood chips and the leaves into compost. Both are free behind the Service Department from May 1 to September 30.
  • Don't want to wait on the truck?Our Fall Pass is one price for the whole leaf season. We keep coming back till it's clear. See the Fall Pass

From the Village of Richfield website, read October 2026. Rules can change, so check there before you put anything at the curb.
