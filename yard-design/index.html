Does Grass 'n' Gardens do custom landscape design?

Yes. We build retaining walls and paver patios and handle full custom landscape design and construction alongside routine lawn and garden care.

How do you pick the plants?

We check how much sun the spot gets, the soil, and how big the plant will grow. Then we pick plants that fit that spot.

Can you move plants that aren't doing well?

Yes. If a plant is in the wrong spot, we can move it to one where it will grow. We just did that with a customer's hydrangeas.
