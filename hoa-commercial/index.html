Questions

Board questions, straight answers.

Don't see yours? Email or text Harrison.

Is Grass 'n' Gardens insured for commercial work?

Yes. Grass 'n' Gardens is insured. Proof of insurance comes with every bid, and we can add your HOA or management company to it.

When should our HOA ask for bids?

Most HOA boards set next year's budget in the fall. Ask for bids in September through November so the new plan can start in spring.

What do you need from us to bid?

A map or list of the areas the HOA takes care of, and a time to walk the property. We price only the common areas, and we put that line in writing.

Will you come to our board meeting?

Yes. Harrison comes to the meeting, shows photos from the walk, and answers questions.

How does billing work?

One price for the year, split into the same bill every month. Add-ons are priced in the bid, so nothing new shows up on a bill without a yes first.
