Try it in your yard

Grab a screwdriver. Push it in.

  1. Wait for a day after rain, or water a spot first. The test needs damp ground.
  2. Push a long screwdriver straight down with one steady hand. Don't lean on it.
  3. See how far it went. Try 3 spots: the front, the back, and where people walk.

Healthy soil lets it slide in 6 inches or more. If it stops at 2 or 3 inches, the soil is packed down. It's a quick check, not a lab test. If you'd rather we do it, ask for a Free Soil Check. A crew member does it when we're on your street, and we text you the photos.

When it happens in Ohio.

  1. Spring (April and May)A light feeding as the grass wakes up. Aeration works in spring too, if it can't wait.
  2. Early fall (late August and September)The best time to aerate and overseed. The ground is warm, nights are cool, and new grass has time to root before winter.
  3. Late fall (November)One more feeding before the cold. The roots store it and the lawn greens up faster in spring.

Most yards around here sit on clay. Clay packs down hard, so we aerate every fall.

When is the best time to aerate a lawn in Ohio?

Early fall, from late August through September. Grass 'n' Gardens aerates and overseeds lawns in Richfield then, because new seed has time to root before winter. Spring is second best.

What is the Free Soil Check?

Text us your address. When a crew is on your street that week, they push a screwdriver into your lawn in 3 spots and text you the photos next to a 6-inch mark. It takes about 5 minutes and you don't have to be home. If your yard passes, we tell you. Get a Free Soil Check

Does aeration leave a mess?

It leaves small plugs of dirt on the lawn. They break down on their own after a few mows and a little rain. We blow them off your drive and walks before we leave.

Do I have to be home?

No. Just tell us about any sprinkler heads or buried lines so we can mark them first.
